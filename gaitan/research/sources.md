# Investigación histórica — Jorge Eliécer Gaitán

Research for a 30-second TikTok documentary. Every claim in the narration is marked ✅ (stated in the video) and cross-checked against at least two independent sources. Disputed points are listed separately and kept out of the narration.

> **Research method and limits.** The build container's network policy blocks Wikipedia/Wikimedia, archive.org, Banrepcultural, Radio Nacional and most news sites, so pages could not be opened directly. Facts were gathered through web-search summaries of the pages linked below and cross-checked between them. Before publishing, a person should open the key pages (especially Banrepcultural, Universidad del Rosario and the Commons licence pages) and confirm them.

## 3. The Banana Massacre debate ✅

- The massacre of striking United Fruit Company workers took place in Ciénaga (Magdalena) on 5–6 December 1928.
- ✅ In September 1929 Gaitán led a multi-day debate in the Cámara (the published transcript covers the sessions of 3–6 September 1929), blaming the army and the company and demanding compensation. It badly damaged the Conservative government of Miguel Abadía Méndez and made him a national figure.
- Sources: [Las2orillas](https://www.las2orillas.co/?p=555967), [transcript *El debate sobre las bananeras* (archive.org)](https://ia802707.us.archive.org/5/items/660033/Gait%C3%A1n.%20El%20debate%20sobre%20las%20bananeras.pdf), [Trashumante (UdeA)](https://revistas.udea.edu.co/index.php/trashumante/article/view/336805/20815436), [Biografías y Vidas](https://www.biografiasyvidas.com/biografia/e/eliecer.htm)
- Death toll of the massacre: **disputed** (from dozens to over a thousand). Not stated in the video.

## 4. Presidential ambitions ✅ ("Muchos lo veían como el próximo presidente")

- 1946: the Liberals split between Gabriel Turbay (official) and Gaitán (dissident); the Conservative Mariano Ospina Pérez won with about 41%. Reported totals: Ospina 565,939, Turbay 441,199, Gaitán 356,995.
- March 1947: Gaitanismo won the congressional elections; Gaitán became *jefe único* of the Liberal Party.
- He was widely seen as the likely Liberal winner of the 1950 election. **He was never president-elect**, so the narration says only that many saw him as the next president.
- 7 February 1948: *Marcha del Silencio* in the Plaza de Bolívar (tens of thousands; estimates vary from ~50,000 to ~100,000), ending with his *Oración por la paz* to President Ospina.
- Sources: [Semana — "Un hombre, un pueblo"](https://www.semana.com/especiales/articulo/un-hombre-pueblo/60095-3/), [Primicias](https://www.primicias.ec/noticias/firmas/lecciones-nos-dejo-el-bogotazo), [Diario del Sur](https://www.diariodelsur.com.co/por-la-vida-y-la-paz/), [Blu Radio / HJCK](https://www.bluradio.com/hjck/la-oracion-por-la-paz-el-discurso-historico-de-jorge-eliecer-gaitan)

## 5. 9 April 1948 ✅

- ✅ Shortly after 1 p.m. (accounts say 1:05 or about 1:15), Gaitán left his office in the Edificio Agustín Nieto, Carrera Séptima at Calle 14 (Avenida Jiménez), central Bogotá, and was shot on the street.
- He was hit by three bullets (accounts differ on how many shots were fired) and taken to the Clínica Central. ✅ He died there that afternoon.
- Juan Roa Sierra, identified by witnesses as the shooter, was lynched by the crowd almost immediately.
- **Who ordered the killing is not established.** Investigations and many theories (government, Liberal rivals, foreign agencies, a lone actor) remain unresolved. The video does not suggest any culprit.
- Sources: [Radio Nacional](https://radionacional.co/cultura/historia-colombiana/quien-mato-jorge-eliecer-gaitan), [El Universal (2018)](https://www.eluniversal.com.co/colombia/2018/04/07/asesinato-de-gaitan-70-anos-del-crimen-que-partio-en-dos-la-historia-colombiana/), [El Universal — "El asesino de Gaitán"](https://www.eluniversal.com.co/suplementos/dominical/2013/04/14/el-asesino-de-gaitan/), [El Tiempo](https://www.eltiempo.com/politica/el-ultimo-juicio-de-jorge-eliecer-gaitan-482924), [UPB](https://plataforma.bucaramanga.upb.edu.co/acontecer/el-bogotazo-74-anos-y-persisten-las-duda)

## 6. The Bogotazo and consequences ✅

- ✅ Rioting broke out within hours: trams and public buildings were burned (one source counts 142 fires in the capital), and much of downtown Bogotá was destroyed in roughly ten hours.
- The IX Pan-American Conference, at which the OAS was founded, was under way in Bogotá at the time.
- Deaths: **no reliable count**; estimates run from about 500 to more than 3,000.
- Unrest spread to other cities. The killing is widely treated as a turning point that deepened *La Violencia*, the partisan violence between Liberals and Conservatives (1946/48–1958). The Liberals abstained from the 1949 presidential election, and Laureano Gómez was elected.
- ✅ "Colombia nunca volvió a ser la misma" is the video's interpretive closing line. It reflects the consensus that 9 April 1948 was a historical rupture; it is not a factual claim about a specific event.
- Sources: [Bogotá.gov.co](https://bogota.gov.co/mi-ciudad/gestion-publica/que-es-el-bogotazo-esto-ocurrio-el-9-de-abril-de-1948), [Bogotá.gov.co — momentos](https://bogota.gov.co/mi-ciudad/bogota-en-historias/jorge-eliecer-gaitan-momentos-antes-y-despues-del-bogotazo-en-1948), [Radio Nacional — resumen](https://radionacional.co/cultura/historia-colombiana/bogotazo-9-de-abril-1948-resumen-causas-y-consecuencias), [Wikipedia — Bogotazo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogotazo), [Wikipedia — La Violencia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Violencia), [Banrepcultural — Sady González, El Bogotazo](https://www.banrepcultural.org/exposiciones/foto-sady/exposicion/el-bogotazo)
